NATO — North Atlantic Treaty Organization

NATO is a political and military alliance established by the North Atlantic Treaty, signed on 4 April 1949.

The founding members were Belgium, Canada, Denmark, France, Iceland, Italy, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

NATO was created to provide collective security for its members. The central principle of the alliance is collective defence: an armed attack against one or more members is considered an attack against them all under Article 5 of the North Atlantic Treaty.

NATO's headquarters is located in Brussels, Belgium.

The organization has evolved since its creation and addresses a range of security and defence issues.
